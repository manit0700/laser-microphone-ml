# Simulator vs. rig — concrete differences, what they bias in Sprint 8, and what to measure

Agent R, 2026-11-08. Companion to `assumptions_review.md` (evidence and citations) and `sources.md` (links). "Sim" = `scripts/physics_laser_sim.py` as it stands; "rig" = the Jetson + PCM1808 + laser receiver described in `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md` Exp 6 (2026-10-12 measurements). Sprint 8 results referred to: A = dataset/manifest, B = reference vs fine-tuned CNN and the condition table (sim_good/medium/poor), C = replay harness.

## A. Differences, ranked by how much they can move a Sprint 8 number

## B. Where the simulator is right (keep)

- LTI resonator form for the surface and erf knife-edge optics are textbook-correct forms (Visual Microphone; knife-edge literature).
- Shot ∝ √I plus white electronic noise is the correct *form* of detector noise (Hamamatsu).
- Hum fundamental at 60 Hz with harmonics and 120 Hz optical flicker are the right ingredients (CEDAR, EE World, Lamphone).
- Hard clipping at full scale is the right shape of ADC overload; the PCM1808's own anti-alias/decimation filter and 99 dB dynamic range mean the ADC is not the limiting block.
- Domain randomisation over alignment and gain is sensible given that the rig's alignment is not under closed-loop control.

## C. Measurements the hardware team should make (each one closes a row above)

1. **Quiet-rig spectrum** (rows 2, 3, 10): 10 s, laser channel, 44.1 kHz, nobody speaking, Welch PSD (8192-pt). Read off: 60 Hz line level, amplitude of each harmonic to 420 Hz, the 120 Hz component, and the broadband floor shape 200–4000 Hz. Repeat with the **beam blocked** (separates optical flicker + shot noise from electrical hum + electronic noise) and with the **laser off** (electronic floor only). Save the three WAVs un-normalised (float32) or note the scale.
2. **Paired utterance set for the SNR prior** (row 1): ≥ 50 digit utterances, ≥ 3 speakers, at the demo distance, `record_daq_wav.py --channel both`. For each clip compute in-band power in speech vs non-speech frames (speech frames chosen on the **std** channel) and SNR = 10·log10((P_s − P_q)/P_q) on the laser channel. Report median, 10th/90th percentile; this histogram replaces U(0, 30). Band-pass both channels before running `--calibrate-from`.
3. **Target frequency response** (row 4): play a 100 Hz–4 kHz swept sine (or white noise) through the rig speaker; |H(f)| = |Laser(f)|/|Mic(f)|. State the target material, size and mounting. If the response is monotone-falling with no in-band peaks, the sim needs the "plate" branch; if it has 1–3 peaks, the current branch is right.
4. **Level and bit-depth check** (rows 8, 11; ADC packing): dump 1 s of raw int32 from `_DAQSource`; confirm the low 8 bits are zero (24-in-32), report raw peak as a fraction of full scale and the count of samples at |x| ≥ 0.999 FS during the loudest utterance.
5. **Receiver schematic numbers** (row 12): coupling capacitor/resistor (HP corner), TIA feedback R and C (LP corner and Johnson noise), gain setting. Also whether the supply is battery or mains (Exp 6 open item 1).
6. **Per-digit detection rate on the rig** (row 9): 10 takes per digit per speaker through `_LiveClassifier(word_mode=True)`; log *detected / not detected* before *correct / incorrect*. Watch "six" and "eight".
7. **Session stability** (row 13): repeat measurement 2 on two different days / after re-alignment; the between-session spread of SNR and |H(f)| tells A how to block the sim parameters.
8. **Linearity of the optics** (knife-edge depth): play a 500 Hz tone at the loudest demo level; compare THD on the laser channel vs the std mic. THD ≪ 10 % means the erf is operating in its linear region and `depth` should be sampled small.

## D. One-paragraph summary for the Sprint 8 report

The simulator has the right physics *forms* at every stage but is miscalibrated against the only rig measurements that exist: its SNR prior is 0–30 dB where the rig sits at 3–7.5 dB in-band; its hum is up to 10 dB too quiet at the fundamental yet leaks ~60× too much harmonic power into the speech band; its surface model places resonances inside the speech band, which fits bags and membranes but not window glass; and it clips 27 % of clips while the rig has not been seen to clip. In addition, the deployed laser path differs from the training path in three ways that are not physics but plumbing — a zero-phase block band-pass applied only live, peak-normalised 16-bit saving of captures, and an absolute-level word gate whose 0.12 s minimum is at the measured duration of "six" — each of which can move a Sprint 8 replay or accuracy number independently of the model. The per-condition sim_poor results and a re-fitted threshold/temperature are the only Sprint 8 numbers that should be quoted as predictive of the rig, and the eight measurements in §C would let the next sprint replace the guessed priors with measured ones.
